A modern retelling of Ovid's myth, made for the Remaking the Renaissance assignment in Renaissance Literature. The whole story plays out as a text exchange you watch as it's typed. Narcissus is stuck on his own curated reflection. Echo can only send his words back, the way an algorithm feeds us what we already believe. I cut it from five takes. Reading someone's texts as they arrive feels intrusive on purpose. It asks whether our private messages will one day be studied the way we study Renaissance letters.
