<Chapter n="01 · Play" title="Evolution" light="you do with your hands">
  <p>Remember the playground toys where you spin a panel to swap an animal's head, body and feet? We built a fighting game around that feeling. Each player gets a controller with three spinning drums: head, torso and legs. Spin one mid-fight and your creature's body swaps on screen. The head sets your attacks, the torso your defense, the legs how you move.</p>
  <Fig img={shot1} alt="The spinning-drum controllers next to gameplay" caption="The controllers and the game" ratio="16/9" />
</Chapter>

<Chapter n="02 · Build" title="Spin" light="to evolve">
  <p>I built the electronics. Each drum hides a ring of IR sensors that read colored zones on the 3D-printed core as it turns, and a Teensy turns those readings into the game's input. The controllers went from cardboard to wood to printed cores with laser-cut panels. I worked closely with Adam on the controller design throughout.</p>
