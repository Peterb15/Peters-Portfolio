<Chapter n="01 · Play" title="Evolution" light="you do with your hands">
  <p>What if evolution wasn't something that happened to you, but something you physically did? Each player gets a controller with three spinning drums: head, torso and legs. Spin one mid-fight and your creature's body swaps on screen, along with its abilities.</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Co-designed the spin-to-evolve interaction</li>
    <li>Engineered the sensing: IR sensors inside multicolored 3D-printed drums read each rotation</li>
    <li>Wrote the Teensy firmware that turns drum positions into game input</li>
    <li>Fabricated the controllers: cardboard → wood → printed cores with laser-cut panels</li>
  </ul>
