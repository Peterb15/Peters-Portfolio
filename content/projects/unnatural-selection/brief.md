## One-line pitch
A two-player arcade fighter where you physically spin your creature's head, torso and legs mid-fight to evolve on the fly.

## Framing
This is your **guest-facing interactive** piece. It's close to what a themed-entertainment interactive or queue game looks like, so lead with people playing it.

## What I did
1. Co-designed the spin-to-evolve interaction
2. Engineered the IR sensor system inside 3D-printed cylinders
3. Wrote the Teensy firmware
4. Fabricated the controllers (cardboard → wood → printed cores + laser-cut panels)
