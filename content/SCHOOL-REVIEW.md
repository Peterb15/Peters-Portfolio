# School folder review

Source: `Dropbox/School/Courses` (29 courses, ~22k files). Read only, nothing modified. Selects are copied to `content/projects/<slug>/media/selects/`.

## Worth adding to the site

### 1. Hole 66, the RC mini-golf hole (Whaaat!? Studio Lab, Fall 2025). **Recommend: Feature**
"Mini golf, but it's RC racing." A Route 66-themed hole for the Whaaat!? Festival 2025 mini-golf course.
- **Concept and prototyping:** golf-club gyro/accelerometer controller → RC cars balancing the ball → ramming. Your notes say "the most fun part was ramming cars into each other."
- **Course design:** written spec with three obstacles (Ramp 1, Ramp 2 with a sand trap, Tilt), a material estimate (91 sq ft, three 4×8 plywood sheets) and track math for 1/24 and 1/16 scale.
- **Build:**
  - Interlocking cardboard prototype track
  - Turf, sand traps and log borders
  - "Get your kicks on HOLE 66" sign and a "HIT THE GAS" road graphic
  - A rules sign
  - 3D-printed custom truck bodies (golf carts)
- **Festival:** videos of kids playing (including "not how intended"), the ramming, a full course run and a cart showcase.
- **Bonus:** the **Whaaat!? Festival Personality Test**, a playable Ink web game. It could be embedded on the site. Merit-badge designs too.
- **Why it matters:** a themed, guest-facing attraction you designed, prototyped, spec'd and built for a real crowd. That's themed-entertainment work.
- **CHECK:** solo or team? Who designed the truck bodies? Credit the studio lead/instructor?

### 2. ARNOLD.EXE (Game Dev, Fall 2024). **Recommend: Archive (narrative design)**
A choice-based narrative game in Godot + Ink. You wake up as an android, ARNOLD, and Teddy, the engineer who made you, talks you through your boot sequence. There's a branching script (~1,150 words in draft 2), a terminal UI and a pixel-art lab scene. A 3.5-minute playthrough video is copied.
- Could be exported for the web and made playable on the site. **CHECK:** do you want that?

### 3. DM Carrying Case (Form, Spring 2024). **Recommend: Archive (fabrication)**
A wooden carrying case with a 3D-printed insert for dice, minis and DM gear, designed in SketchUp with 12 dimensioned layout sheets plus a reflection PDF. Ties to Shattered Lands.
- **Need:** a photo of the finished case. The folder only has drawings.

### 4. Balance Board Pong (Alt Arcades, Fall 2024). **Recommend: Archive**
Pong played by standing on a balance board with an MPU-6050 tilt sensor → Arduino → Godot. A 42-second play video is copied.

### 5. Secrets That Run the World (English capstone, Spring 2024). **Recommend: add to the Shattered Lands page**
A one-of-one artifact book and conspiracy board set in Pangros, about propaganda and rewritten history. It has burned and aged papers, yarn, and a stone tablet modeled in Adobe Dimension.
- The colophon says **"Published by Players Win Ties LLC."** That's great proof that the world reaches beyond the show.

### 6. The Cthulhu Journals (Comics, Fall 2022). **Recommend: Writing section or archive**
Six pencil comic pages with an artist statement. A cosmic being, Aylith, pulls characters between panels and breaks the comic's own rules. Same world as your "Cthulhu Journals" novel notes.
