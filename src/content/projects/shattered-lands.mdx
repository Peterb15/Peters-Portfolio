<Chapter n="01 · World" title="Welcome to" light="Pangros">
  <p>Pangros is an ocean of broken islands circling a cyclone that has never stopped. It pulls from a lot of things I love: the wonder of One Piece, Critical Role's Calamity, and Worlds Beyond Number's <i>The Wizard, the Witch and the Wild One</i> above all, plus steampunk, Tolkien, Treasure Planet and adventure stories in general.</p>
  > I wanted a world that begs to be explored, where every island holds a mystery and anything could be possible.
  <p>I also wanted something other people could add to. So as I wrote the lore, geography, factions and history, I worked with each player to build their character's culture, race and backstory deep into the world.</p>

<Chapter n="02 · Set" title="A room" light="you sit inside">
  <p>I designed a film set that puts the players on the deck of a ship: plank walls, fishing nets, and a painted DM screen with a ship sailing into the sun. At the center is a table we built for the shoot. It's shaped so the cameras can catch me and every player from the angles we needed, with an inlay in the middle where the battle sets drop in. Three to four cameras and a boom covered the table, and an overhead rig caught the fights.</p>

<Chapter n="03 · Battle sets" title="The Last" light="Bastion">

The Last Bastion is the biggest of them: a walled harbor city of towers, a tavern and lit streets, with rooms that open up for fights inside. B-roll of each set is filmed separately and cut in during post.</p>

<Chapter n="05 · Production" title="Running" light="the show">
  <p>Episode 1 is complete. Episodes 2 through 5 are in various stages of editing. Kaeden Tilak wrote a theme for every character, from "The Ship Sails!" to "The Storm Surges" for the Pirate King, and recorded the score live with a full band. At its peak, 25 people worked on the show across editing, music, art, sets, film and writing. I ran it with a production tracker, regular editing meetings and a written post-production workflow.</p>

<Chapter n="06 · Beyond the table" title="A world" light="people build together">
  <p>The Shattered Lands grew past the episodes. Everyone pitched in to build the battle sets, from the first cardboard city to the finished Last Bastion. We did a photo shoot with the players in costume as their characters. I've written short stories set in Pangros, and my English capstone, "Secrets That Run the World," is a one-of-one artifact from inside the world: a conspiracy board of burned and aged papers and yarn that follows one person as the history they were taught comes apart. It's about how propaganda turns history into a weapon.</p>
