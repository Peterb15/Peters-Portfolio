<Chapter n="01 · World" title="Welcome to" light="Pangros">
  <p>Pangros is an ocean of broken islands circling a cyclone that has never stopped. The idea came from two places: how much I love collaborative storytelling, and the sense of wonder in One Piece. I wanted a world that begs to be explored, where every island holds a mystery. I wrote the lore, geography, factions and history, then handed the story to five players and a set of dice.</p>

<Chapter n="02 · Set" title="A room" light="you sit inside">
  <p>The players didn't sit at a kitchen table. I designed a filming set that put them on the deck of a ship: plank walls, fishing nets, a painted DM screen with a ship sailing into the sun, and a sculpted sea where the fights happen. The crew built it. Three to four cameras and a boom covered the table, and an overhead rig caught the battle maps.</p>

<Chapter n="05 · Production" title="Running" light="the show">
  <p>Eight sessions are filmed: about fifteen hours of footage across up to four cameras. Episode 1 is frame-locked. Episodes 2 through 5 are in rough cut. Kaeden Tilak wrote a theme for every character, from "The Ship Sails!" to "The Storm Surges" for the Pirate King. At its peak, 25 people worked on the show across editing, music, art, sets, film and writing. I ran it with a production tracker, monthly meetings and a written post-production workflow.</p>
  <Fig img={overheadSea} alt="Players leaning over the sea battle set mid-session" caption="Mid-session on the sea set" ratio="21/9" />
</Chapter>

<Chapter n="06 · Beyond the table" title="Secrets that" light="run the world">
  <p>Pangros doesn't only live on camera. For my English capstone I made a one-of-one artifact book set in the world: a conspiracy board of burned, crumpled and aged pages, yarn and a stone tablet, following one person whose beliefs come apart when they discover that the history they were taught was rewritten. It's about how propaganda uses history as a weapon. It was published under Players Win Ties, the company name behind the show.</p>

<Chapter n="07 · Writing" title="The world," light="on paper">
  <p>Pangros existed in writing before it existed on a table: a world bible of islands, peoples, gods and factions, plus pieces written from inside the world. The history book and the artifact report tell two different stories about the same war, and that disagreement is the point.</p>
