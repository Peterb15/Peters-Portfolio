<Chapter n="02 · Pipeline" title="Built in" light="Blender">
  <ul>
    <li>Modeled the robot from primitives and modifiers, with a lot of small mechanical detail</li>
    <li>Rigged with IK legs, FK arms and control bones for the head and spine</li>
    <li>Animated blocking → spline → polish, working on the weight of the walk cycle</li>
    <li>Scattered rubble with Geometry Nodes, weight-painted to keep the path clear</li>
    <li>Procedural metal, rust and stone materials. HDRI plus area lights, rendered in Cycles, cut in Premiere</li>
  </ul>
  <p>The plan was two scenes. Halfway through I cut to one so the approach and the rewind could get real polish. That's the same call I made on Echo a year later.</p>
