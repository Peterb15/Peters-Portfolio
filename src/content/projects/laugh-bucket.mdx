<Chapter n="01 · Idea" title="Fill the" light="bucket">
  <p>I wanted something strange and attention-grabbing that left people confused but intrigued. The Laugh Bucket is a retro TV cabinet with a big button and a microphone. Press the button, laugh when it lights up, and your laugh goes into the bucket. On the screen you can watch a live feed of a living room down the hall. When the bucket is full, you pull the lever and every laugh it's collected gets dumped on whoever is sitting there.</p>

<Chapter n="02 · Build" title="How it" light="works">
  <ul>
    <li>Arduino inputs trigger recording and playback, layering each new laugh over the last</li>
    <li>NDI carries the audio and the live video between the two rooms in real time</li>
    <li>I designed and fabricated the cabinet, control box and display stand</li>
  </ul>

<Chapter n="03 · Festival" title="People came" light="back for more">
  <p>The most interesting part was how people's behavior changed over the festival. Once visitors figured out what it did, many came back to record more laughs and used them to ambush whoever sat on the couch next.</p>
