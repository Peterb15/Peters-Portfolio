How do you cope with reality once you realize you're an ant on a circuit board? Aylith, a cosmic being, warps the rules of the comic itself: she drags characters between panels, and as they start to understand their world they begin to glance at the reader. Each character has a color and an icon (a greatsword, a gun, a camera, a baseball) so a large cast reads at a glance.

The comic comes out of a novel I've been writing in the same world, and a short story in the same Lovecraftian territory.
