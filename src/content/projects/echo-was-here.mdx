<Chapter n="01 · Character" title="Meet" light="Echo">
  <p>Echo is 22 and lives in the poverty apartments of Notting, a city we based on Chicago. She plays every instrument herself because she refuses to need a band. Rent is due, guitars cost money, so she robs banks on roller skates and leaves her tag behind. She's cheered on by locals as she skates by.</p>

  > Everyone has a side hustle. Mine's just more fun.

<Chapter n="02 · Directing" title="Cut to" light="the song">
  <p>Once the song arrived, every edit decision answered to it. I built the animatic in Blender's VSE, timing every shot to the frame against the track. The first 17 seconds play in total silence while Echo suits up. The music hits on the mirror reveal (shot 018), the full band drops at the skate launch (023), and the solo breakdown lands on the guitar shot (040).</p>
  <p>I cut the apartment intro and the aftermath to put everything into the heist itself.</p>

<Chapter n="03 · Pipeline" title="Building" light="the look">
  <ul>
    <li><b>Cel-shading:</b> Diffuse → Shader to RGB → three-step ColorRamp → Emission in EEVEE, with Grease Pencil line art per shot.</li>
    <li><b>Rig:</b> retopologized Raphaelle's sculpt of the body, face and hair, Rigify body, and a face rig that swaps hand-drawn eye and mouth sprites through drivers, so expressions keyframe like poses.</li>
    <li><b>Modeling:</b> all of the clothing, including the heist gear (jacket, goggles, skates, guards), and all four instruments. The layered clothing broke normal weight painting until Voxel Heat Diffuse Skinning solved it.</li>
    <li><b>Shots:</b> every shot links the rig through Library Overrides. A $30 bank asset pack replaced an estimated 15+ hours of environment modeling.</li>
  </ul>

<Chapter n="04 · Physical" title="Music you" light="can play with">
  <p>Every blind box holds one of four figurines. Set her in a slot on the speaker and her part of "Breaking In" plays on its own: vocals, guitar, bass or drums. Collect the band and the whole song builds. I posed Echo from the animation rig for each figurine and for the table-size statue. Zeke built the RFID speaker, and the team printed and airbrushed every piece.</p>

<Chapter n="06 · Show design" title="The booth was" light="her apartment">
  <p>At the ATLAS EXPO we didn't set up a table. We dressed the space as Echo's apartment: a drum kit and amps, a leather couch, a record player, a merch rack with her shirts, the hand-painted banner, the blind-box tower, the money bag and her roller skates. The film ran on a TV in the middle of it. Visitors walked into her world before they knew who she was.</p>

<Chapter n="07 · Notes" title="What the experts" light="said">
  <p>We pitched Echo to people who make this for a living. The Design Director at Toys for Bob, the studio behind Skylanders, told us the products that last have three things: accessibility, ease of use and one design across every medium. He also told us to establish the rules of the world. A consumer-products veteran pushed us to think about the chase: rarity, ratios and the one you can't find.</p>
  <p class="soft">What's next: the capstone team has moved on, but I'm continuing Echo on my own, starting with the exterior backgrounds.</p>
</Chapter>
