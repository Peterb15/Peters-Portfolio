A modern retelling of Ovid's Narcissus and Echo, told entirely through a text exchange you watch as it's typed. Narcissus is stuck on his own curated reflection. Echo can only send his words back, the way an algorithm feeds us what we already believe. I cut the piece from five takes. Reading someone's texts as they arrive feels intrusive on purpose. It raises the question of whether our private messages will be studied one day the way we study Renaissance letters.
