Designed in SketchUp around the gear I actually bring to the Shattered Lands table: a 3D-printed inner casing with fitted pockets for dice and minis, inside a hand-built wooden shell. My first big 3D print taught me to keep printing small. The full insert took so long to print that half of it couldn't finish on time, and I switched the shell from laser-cut to woodworking to carry the weight.
