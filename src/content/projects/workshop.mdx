<Chapter n="04 · Woodworking" title="The" light="bookshelf">
  <p>My Material Studies final, and a piece I actually wanted to use. It's inspired by Charlotte Perriand's modular bookshelves and her idea that "the driving force behind creation is need." It's built in poplar with box joints left visible on the front, so the handwork shows, and the rest of the joinery is hidden. The second material was meant to be welded metal. When I couldn't get into the metal shop, I pivoted to acrylic: laser cut, then heat-molded into the backboards and details. I modeled the whole thing in Onshape first to plan every cut.</p>
