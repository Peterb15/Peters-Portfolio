## The pitch
A D&D actual-play series from my production label, **Players Win Ties**. It's set in Pangros, a world of broken islands circling a permanent cyclone. I built the world, ran the table as DM, led the crew, and built a themed filming set and battle sets for the players to fight on.

Your own definition (from `What is D&D Actual Play.pdf`):
> "…no one has true control over the story or the characters… a story crafted by the will of many instead of just one."

## Story of the project (case study sections)
1. **The world.** Pangros world map (and a second version by Moe), five player characters:
   - Atlas: Mist Genasi Wizard, played by Jacob Monroe
   - Illvana: Changeling Rogue, played by Mariah Ruff
   - Mortimer: Human Paladin, played by Avery Krier
   - Quinn: Half-Elf Fighter, played by Rowan Wileke
   - Salu: Aarakocra Cleric/Ranger, played by Kaeden Tilak
2. **The set.** A themed filming space with:
   - A ship-plank backdrop and fishing nets
   - A painted DM screen with a ship at sunset
   - An overhead camera on a rig
   - A sea battle table with hand-sculpted ocean terrain, a printed and painted galleon, and a leviathan skeleton ship
   - **Sets: designed by me, built by the crew** (credit the build team). **DM screen: designed by me, made by me and Moe Collins.**
3. **Battle sets + minis.**
   - Printed and painted ships and characters from licensed STLs (modular warship, leviathan ship, pirates, cultists, sahuagin…)
   - Planned sets: Last Bastion, Mount Agrobora (torii gates), red-rock desert
   - Minis shot on the DM-screen backdrop look great
   - Minis painted by **Mariah Ruff**
4. **The score.** Kaeden Tilak's themes, recorded in a Feb 17 2025 session (with BTS video):
   - The Ship Sails! (ship's theme)
   - Blood Red Dust (Quinn)
   - Dream from the Mountainpeak (Salu)
   - Two-Headed Serpent (Mortimer)
   - Swaying Palm Trees (Atlas)
   - Of the Crypt (Illvana)
   - Tower & Circle (Cerelia)
   - The Storm Surges (Pirate King)
5. **The broadcast package.**
   - Stat-card overlays for each character (your task in the tracker) and character art
   - PWT logo, animated intro, credits
   - Episode intros (your tasks)
6. **Running post-production.**
   - Ep 1 is frame-locked (export Dec 2025)
   - Eps 2–5 are in rough cut; Ep 4 B-roll was shot Feb 2026
   - Editing meetings Jan–Jul 2025, a task tracker, and a written post workflow
