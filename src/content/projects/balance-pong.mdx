An MPU-6050 tilt sensor under a balance board reads how far you lean. An Arduino sends that over serial to a Pong game in Godot, so your paddle is your balance. Built for Alt Arcades, the class where I also made Laugh Bucket and Unnatural Selection.

<Clip src="/video/pong.mp4" poster={still} ratio="9/16" maxWidth="420px" caption="Playing it" />

It started as a page of thirty ideas for a strange Pong controller. The balance board won.
