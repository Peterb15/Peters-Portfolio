<Chapter n="01 · Electronics" title="D&D" light="Stat Tracker">
  <p>A handheld tracker for hit points and spell slots at the table: an OLED for HP and spells, an LCD for character info and a joystick for input, hand-soldered to a protoboard in a laser-cut, engraved wood enclosure with room for a battery. My first fully documented electronics project, written up step by step on Instructables.</p>
